About BrightSteps

Where every child is known, valued, and challenged

Our mission is to nurture lifelong learners and global citizens through a high quality, student centered education that inspires curiosity, builds confidence, and empowers every child to reach their full potential.

Our Approach

An inquiry driven education

Our program combines inquiry based learning with the clarity and structure of the Common Core State Standards, taught in English with French as a strong second language.

Inquiry Based Learning

Students ask questions, explore ideas, and make real world connections rather than memorize facts.

Common Core Rigor

A clear, structured curriculum builds strong foundations in literacy, mathematics, and critical thinking.

Bilingual by Design

English medium instruction paired with French as a second language from the earliest years.
