Our approach

An inquiry driven education

Our program combines inquiry based learning with the clarity and structure of rigorous academic standards, taught in English with French as a strong second language.

  • Inquiry based learning

    Students ask questions, explore ideas, and make real world connections rather than memorize facts.

  • Academic rigor

    A clear, structured curriculum builds strong foundations in literacy, mathematics, and critical thinking.

  • A strong foundation in French

    English medium instruction paired with French as a second language from the earliest years.
