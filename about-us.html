About BrightSteps

Where every child is known, valued, and challenged

BrightSteps International School is an English medium primary school in Grand Bassam, where students are inspired to think, explore, and grow within a caring, globally minded community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to grow with our students, expanding year by year toward Grade 12, while staying true to what matters most, a joyful, purposeful education that prepares students for life.

Our Mission

We nurture lifelong learners and global citizens through a high quality, student centered education that inspires curiosity, builds confidence, and empowers every child to reach their full potential. Inquiry based learning paired with the rigor of the Common Core State Standards encourages students to think critically, ask questions, and make connections to the world around them.

As an English medium school with French as a second language, we equip students with the knowledge, skills, and multilingual ability to thrive in an interconnected world, while building the character, compassion, and responsibility of future global citizens.

Our School

Located in Mockeyville, next to the Chateau d'Eau in Grand Bassam, BrightSteps offers a safe, nurturing, and engaging environment where children thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.

We invite you to visit our campus, meet our team, and discover how BrightSteps is helping students become confident learners and global citizens.

Our Team

The heart of BrightSteps is our dedicated team of educators. United by a shared commitment to excellence, our teachers foster a nurturing, engaging environment where students think critically, ask questions, and build confidence in their abilities.

Through collaboration, continuous professional development, and strong partnerships with families, we ensure that every child is known, supported, and inspired to achieve their best.
