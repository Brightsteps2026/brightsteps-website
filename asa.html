School Life

After School Activities

Our After School Activities program offers students from Kindergarten through Grade 7 a variety of engaging, age appropriate activities designed to inspire curiosity, creativity, and personal growth beyond the classroom.

Art and Craft

Creating and experimenting using a variety of artistic techniques and materials.

Swimming

Water confidence, safety awareness, and swimming skills in a supportive environment.

STEM Explorers

Science, technology, engineering, and math through hands on exploration.

Gardening

Planting, tending, and harvesting in our own school garden.

Music

Singing, instruments, rhythm, and musical expression in a joyful, creative setting.

Robotics

Building, creating, and programming, with an introduction to coding and problem solving.

Dance

Creativity, coordination, and confidence through movement and performance.

Chess

Strategic thinking, concentration, and problem solving through the game of chess.

Soccer

Teamwork, coordination, and sportsmanship through fun, age appropriate play.

Baking

Creativity and practical skills while preparing simple, delicious treats.

Activities rotate each trimester and may vary based on student interest, availability, and enrollment.
