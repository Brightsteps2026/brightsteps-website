School Life

School Uniform

Our school uniform reflects our commitment to a respectful, inclusive, and welcoming learning community, building a sense of belonging, pride, and unity among our students.

School Polo Shirt

10,000 FCFA each, available in white, sky blue, red, and mustard yellow.

PE Sports Jersey

12,000 FCFA each. Required on Physical Education days and must be worn with sneakers.

Uniform Collection

  • School Polo Shirt, worn daily and featuring the BIS logo, 10,000 FCFA each
  • PE Sports Jersey, worn on Physical Education days and school sporting events, 12,000 FCFA each

Guidelines

Polo shirts and PE jerseys are available from the school's Administration Office. Bottoms (skirts, shorts, or pants in khaki or navy blue) are not sold by the school and are purchased independently. Skirts, skorts, and shorts should be no shorter than 5 cm above the knee. We recommend purchasing several tops for a clean uniform throughout the week. All uniform items should be purchased before the start of the school year.

Ordering

Uniforms can be purchased directly from the Administration Office before the school year begins, or throughout the year, subject to availability.
