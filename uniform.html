School Uniform

Our school uniform reflects our commitment to a respectful, inclusive, and welcoming learning community, building a sense of belonging, pride, and unity among our students.

School Polo Shirt

PE Sports Jersey

Uniform Collection

  • School Polo Shirt, worn daily and featuring the BIS logo
  • PE Sports Jersey, worn on Physical Education days and school sporting events

Guidelines

Polo shirts and PE jerseys are available from the school's Administration Office. Uniform tops are paired with khaki or navy blue trousers, shorts, skirts, or skorts purchased independently. Skirts, skorts, and shorts should be no shorter than 5 cm above the knee. We recommend purchasing several tops for a clean uniform throughout the week.

Ordering

Uniforms can be purchased directly from the Administration Office before the school year begins, or throughout the year, subject to availability.
