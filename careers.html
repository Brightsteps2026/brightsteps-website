Careers at BrightSteps

Grow With Us. Make a Difference.

BrightSteps International School is building a team of passionate and dedicated educators who believe that every child deserves to feel known, valued, supported and inspired. We welcome professionals who are ready to contribute their experience, ideas and energy to a growing international school community.

Why Work at BrightSteps?

At BrightSteps, educators have the opportunity to contribute to a purposeful, caring and collaborative learning environment. We value professional growth, creativity, reflective practice and teamwork. Our educators work together to provide engaging, inquiry-based learning experiences that support each child's academic, social and emotional development.

The Educators We Are Looking For

We are looking for educators who are:

  • Passionate about teaching and committed to student growth
  • Caring, patient and attentive to the needs of every child
  • Skilled at creating engaging, inquiry-based learning experiences
  • Curious, reflective and committed to continuous professional learning
  • Collaborative and willing to contribute to the wider school community
  • Respectful of different cultures, languages and perspectives
  • Flexible, resourceful and ready to grow with a developing international school
  • Strong communicators who demonstrate professionalism and integrity
  • Committed to safeguarding and promoting the well-being of every student

Teacher Requirements

Candidates for teaching positions should have:

  • A bachelor's degree in Education or a relevant subject area
  • A recognised teaching qualification
  • Relevant classroom experience
  • Strong spoken and written English
  • An understanding of child-centred and inquiry-based learning
  • The ability to plan, assess, differentiate and communicate effectively
  • A commitment to collaboration and continuous professional development

Learning Assistant Requirements

Candidates for Learning Assistant positions should demonstrate:

  • A genuine interest in working with children
  • Patience, care, reliability and professionalism
  • The ability to support individual students and small groups
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills
  • A willingness to work under the direction of the classroom teacher
  • A commitment to student safety, inclusion and well-being

Previous experience in a school or early childhood setting is an advantage.

Safeguarding

BrightSteps International School is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children. All successful candidates will be required to complete appropriate identity, qualification, reference and background checks before their appointment is confirmed. Candidates must be willing to uphold the school's safeguarding policies and professional code of conduct.

How to Apply

Please submit the following to careers@bischoolci.org:

  • A current CV
  • A cover letter
  • Copies of relevant qualifications
  • Contact details for professional references

Please include the position title in the email subject line. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for an interview.
