School Life

School Canteen

Nourishing the body is an essential part of supporting children's overall wellbeing and learning. Our on site canteen provides balanced, wholesome meals prepared with care in a safe and welcoming environment.

Hearty Mains

Freshly prepared main dishes, made on campus with quality ingredients.

Wholesome Sides

Sweet potato fries and crispy bites, served with a light dipping sauce.

Homemade Treats

Homemade treats and smoothies, a favorite during break time.

Balanced, Wholesome Meals

Our in house chef prepares balanced and flavorful meals using fresh, quality ingredients, including seasonal fruits and herbs grown in our own school garden.

Flexible Meal Options

Families may provide a packed lunch from home, or select our freshly prepared school lunch made on campus. During break times, students can also enjoy healthy snacks including fresh fruit, homemade treats, and smoothies.

View this week's menu

Food Safety and Dietary Considerations

Our canteen follows careful food preparation, hygiene, and safety practices. Families are encouraged to share allergies or dietary requirements so we can support each child's needs.
