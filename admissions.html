Tuition may be paid in full at registration, or divided evenly into 2, 3, or 4 installments, whichever suits your family best. All fees are quoted in FCFA and may be paid by Mobile Money, Wave, Orange Money, cash, check, or bank transfer. Checks should be made payable to BRIGHTSTEPS INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL.
