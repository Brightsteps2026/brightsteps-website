Flexible Payment Plans

Tuition can be paid in full, or divided into 2, 3, or 4 installments. All fees are quoted in FCFA and may be paid by Mobile Money, Wave, Orange Money, cash, check, or bank transfer. Checks should be made payable to BRIGHTSTEPS INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL. Contact our admissions office for the full installment schedule.
