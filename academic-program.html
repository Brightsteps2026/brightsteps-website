Academic Program

An engaging, student centered education

Our curriculum is aligned with the U.S. Common Core State Standards and delivered through an inquiry based approach that encourages students to explore ideas, ask meaningful questions, and apply their learning to real world situations.

As an English medium school, English is the language of instruction across all subjects, while French is taught to build bilingual communication and cultural awareness. Students follow a well rounded curriculum that includes:

  • English Language Arts
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Social Studies
  • The Arts
  • Physical Education
  • Technology
  • Social Emotional Learning
  • Life Skills

Social Emotional Learning

Our Social Emotional Learning program helps students develop self awareness, empathy, resilience, emotional regulation, and positive relationships.

Life Skills

Our Life Skills program builds essential competencies such as communication, leadership, collaboration, financial literacy, problem solving, and responsible decision making, preparing students to become confident, compassionate global citizens.
