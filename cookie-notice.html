Cookie Notice

Last updated: August 2026. This notice explains our current, actual use of cookies on this website.

Starter draft — pending final legal review. This page accurately reflects the website's current, real use of cookies and similar technologies. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Do we use cookies?

This website does not currently set analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of its own, and we do not currently use a cookie-consent banner because no such cookies are set on arrival.

Where cookies could come from

Two parts of the site load resources from outside services, which could set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts — loads typefaces from Google's servers on every page.
  • YouTube — a parent testimonial video embedded on our homepage. YouTube/Google may set cookies once you press play, in line with their own cookie and privacy practices.

If we add analytics, advertising, or other cookie-setting tools in the future, we will update this notice and add a consent mechanism before those cookies are set, in line with applicable rules.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies from YouTube may prevent the embedded video from playing correctly.
