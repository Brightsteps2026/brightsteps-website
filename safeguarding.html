Safeguarding

Last updated: October 2026. This page outlines our safeguarding commitment and how we use images of students.

Our commitment

BrightSteps International School is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of every child in our care. The safety and wellbeing of students comes first in every decision we make.

Designated Safeguarding Lead

Patrick Asseu is the Designated Safeguarding Lead at BrightSteps and is responsible for safeguarding matters at the school. He can be contacted at governance@bischoolci.org.

Reporting a concern

If you have a concern about the safety or wellbeing of a child, please contact our Designated Safeguarding Lead directly at governance@bischoolci.org, or speak to any member of staff, who will pass your concern on appropriately. In an emergency, please contact local emergency services.

Staff recruitment and background checks

All successful candidates are required to complete appropriate identity, qualification, reference, and background checks before their appointment is confirmed. Every member of staff is expected to uphold the school's safeguarding policies and professional code of conduct.

Use of children's images

From time to time, we may photograph or film students for use on this website, our social media pages, and in printed school materials, to celebrate school life and share it with our community.

  • We only use images of a child publicly with the consent of a parent or guardian, collected through the school's enrollment paperwork or a specific consent request.
  • Parents or guardians may withdraw consent for their child's image to be used at any time by contacting info@bischoolci.org.
  • We do not publish a child's full name alongside their photo on public facing pages without separate parental consent.

Online safety

Students use devices and online learning tools, including the Epic reading platform, only under the supervision of an adult. Parents access the BrightSteps Hub with their own login.
