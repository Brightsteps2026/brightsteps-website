Academic Approach

Learning fueled by curiosity and purpose

Education should do more than teach facts. It should inspire students to ask questions, explore ideas, and discover their own potential.

Our Curriculum Design

Our program combines an inquiry driven approach to learning with the clarity and structure of rigorous academic standards. This blend helps students build strong skills in reading, math, and critical thinking while developing creativity, independence, and a global perspective.

English is our main language of instruction, and French is taught as a subject to build strong communication skills in French and cultural understanding. We respect each student's unique path with personalized support and a mindset of growth and resilience.

How inquiry works in our classrooms

Every unit follows the same cycle, so students learn not just what to think but how to find things out.

  1. 1

    Wonder

    Students start with a real question about the world around them.

  2. 2

    Explore

    They investigate through reading, hands on activities, and conversation.

  3. 3

    Create

    They show what they have learned by making, writing, or building something.

  4. 4

    Reflect

    They look back on what worked, what surprised them, and what they would change.

  5. 5

    Act

    They use their learning to make a difference in class, at home, or in the community.

Learning at every stage

Early Childhood Center

Pre Nursery, Pre Kindergarten, and Kindergarten students learn through play, exploration, and discovery in a warm, safe environment. English is woven into daily routines through stories, songs, and meaningful interactions.

Primary Years

Grades 1 through 5 build strong foundations in literacy and mathematics, and explore Science and Social Studies through our Units of Inquiry, while strengthening communication, collaboration, and problem solving.

Middle School

Grades 6 and 7 deepen knowledge across every subject while building the confidence, character, and independence needed for high school and beyond.

Beyond the classroom

Swimming and Music are part of the curriculum

Alongside the Arts, Physical Education, and Technology, every student takes part in Swimming and Music.

In our campus pool, students build water confidence and safety awareness with close adult support. In Music, they explore rhythm, singing, and instruments, including traditional drums.
