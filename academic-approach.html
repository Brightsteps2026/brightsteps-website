Academic Approach

Learning fueled by curiosity and purpose

Education should do more than teach facts. It should inspire students to ask questions, explore ideas, and discover their own potential.

Our Curriculum Design

Our program combines an inquiry driven approach to learning with the clarity and structure of the Common Core State Standards. This blend helps students build strong skills in reading, math, and critical thinking while developing creativity, independence, and a global perspective.

English is our main language of instruction, and French is taught as a subject to build bilingual communication and cultural understanding. We respect each student's unique path with personalized support and a mindset of growth and resilience.

Early Childhood Center

Pre Nursery, Pre Kindergarten, and Kindergarten students learn through play, exploration, and discovery in a warm, safe environment. English is woven into daily routines through stories, songs, and meaningful interactions.

Primary School

Grades 1 through 5 build strong foundations in literacy, mathematics, science, and the social sciences, while strengthening communication, collaboration, and problem solving.

Middle School

Grades 6 and 7 deepen knowledge across every subject while building the confidence, character, and independence needed for high school and beyond.
